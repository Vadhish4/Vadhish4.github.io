Our Story

           
                               
                   

The Sacred Brew for Energy and Life

                   

KASOMA is more than a name—it's a philosophy. "Ka" represents origin and curiosity in Sanskrit; "Soma" was the divine elixir of life in ancient Vedas.

                   

Born in Gujarat, educated in New Jersey, and now rooted back in India, KASOMA blends global wellness with traditional values to fuel every individual's best day.

                   

We source our beans directly from the finest estates across Kerala, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka, honoring India's rich coffee heritage while delivering exceptional quality in every cup.

                   

Each brew is crafted with intention, offering not just coffee, but a divine experience of energy, purity, and life—the essence of KASOMA.
